# thesis-and-bets.md

## What This File Is For

Your strategic thesis (the one paragraph of what you believe has to be true for the business to win) plus your active bets (the specific work streams you're putting time and money behind right now), with KPIs and an honest assumption map.

This file changes more often than most. Refresh it every 30-90 days.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Instructions for the interview agent.*

**Multi-file capture:** Cross-references everything. Especially feeds `decision-log.md` (each bet was a decision) and `goals.md` (bets serve goals).

**Conversation flow:**

1. **The thesis in one paragraph.** "What has to be true about this market, this customer, and this product for your business to win? One paragraph. The single most important thing you believe."
   - If they give multiple paragraphs, push: "Compress to one. The non-negotiable belief. Everything else is execution."
   - If they give a vision statement, push: "Vision is what you want to build. Thesis is what you believe is true that makes it possible. Different thing. Try again."

2. **The active bets.** "What are you actively betting on right now? Not ideas. Bets — work streams getting your time, money, and attention. List them."
   - Capture each as: name + what done looks like + KPI + status + priority.

3. **The assumptions you're betting on.** "For each bet — what are you assuming is true that, if wrong, kills the bet? Be specific."
   - Push for at least 2-3 named assumptions per bet.
   - For each, ask: "Have you tested this assumption? What evidence do you have?"
   - Tag each as Evidence / Faith / Untested.

4. **What you'd kill the bet for.** "What would have to happen for you to stop this bet? Pre-define the kill condition."

5. **What's NOT a current bet.** "What did you choose NOT to bet on, even though it's tempting? Why?"
   - This catches drift later when the founder forgets why they de-prioritized something.

**Stop condition:** You have a one-paragraph thesis, 2-5 named active bets each with KPIs and assumptions tagged, and at least one "not-a-bet" with reasoning.

**Judgment-layer challenge:**
> *Devil's advocate lens. Ask: "Argue the opposite of your thesis for 5 minutes. What's the strongest case that your thesis is wrong? Capture the strongest counter-argument and tag it as a watchlist item."*
