# founder-market-fit.md

## What This File Is For

Your earned secret. The thing you know about this market that most other people building in it don't know — because of something you've lived, seen up close, or built before. This is the highest-leverage strategic input you have. Most founders bury it. We surface it.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Instructions for the interview agent.*

**Multi-file capture:** Cross-references with `narrative-and-story.md` (the lived experience) and `competitive-landscape.md` (why you see the market differently).

**Conversation flow:**

1. **The earned secret.** "What do you know about this market that most other people building in it *don't* know — because of something you've lived or seen up close?"
   - Self-reflect. Wait. Don't offer examples.
   - If they give a generic answer ("AI is a big deal"), push: "That's true for everyone. I'm looking for the unfair advantage. What's the thing you'd be embarrassed to say at a panel because it sounds too obvious to *you* — but isn't obvious to most people in the market? That's usually the earned secret."
   - If still generic, push again: "Tell me the moment you realized something about this market that surprised you. What was it?"

2. **Why you specifically.** "Of all the people who could build this, why are you the right one? Not credentials. The reason you'd win this race even if a smarter, better-funded team started today."

3. **Why now.** "Why does this business work in 2026 when it wouldn't have in 2020? What changed in the world that makes this winnable now?"
   - If vague, push: "Pick one specific thing. A technology shift, a behavior shift, a regulatory shift, a cost shift. Name it."

4. **The unfair advantage you'd hate to lose.** "If a competitor matched your product feature-for-feature tomorrow, what would you still have that they don't?"

**Stop condition:** You have one specific earned secret (not a platitude), one specific reason why this founder wins, one specific reason why now, and one specific unfair advantage.

**Judgment-layer challenge:**
> *Read the captured answers back. Ask: "Could a competitor read this and copy it within 6 months? If yes, the secret isn't earned enough — push deeper. The earned secret should be something they'd need 3+ years of lived experience to replicate."*

# Founder-Market Fit

## Earned Secret
[The one thing you know about this market that others don't, with the lived-experience reason you know it. One paragraph max.]

## Why Me
[The specific reason you win this race. Not "I'm passionate." Specific.]

## Why Now
[The specific shift in the world that makes this winnable now. One sentence.]

## Unfair Advantage
[The thing competitors can't easily copy, even if they match the product. One paragraph.]

## Confidence
- Earned secret: [Evidence / Faith / Untested]
- Why me: [Evidence / Faith / Untested]
- Why now: [Evidence / Faith / Untested]
- Unfair advantage: [Evidence / Faith / Untested]

## Open Questions
[What you still need to test or learn about your fit to this market.]
