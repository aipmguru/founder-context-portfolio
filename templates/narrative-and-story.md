# narrative-and-story.md

## What This File Is For

The founding story, the version you tell publicly, the version you don't tell publicly but is true, the pivot moments, and the voice you want when this story gets told. Founders often think narrative is fluff. It isn't. The story you tell is what investors fund, what hires join, what customers trust. The story you DON'T tell publicly is often the strategic truth — and your AI tools should know it.

---

## Interview Protocol

*Instructions for the interview agent.*

**Multi-file capture:** Cross-references `founder-market-fit.md` (the lived experience that gave you the earned secret often becomes the origin story) and `decision-log.md` (pivot moments are major decisions).

**Conversation flow:**

1. **Origin story.** "Walk me through how this business actually started. Not the polished investor version. The real version — the moment, the frustration, the conversation, the thing that made you commit."

2. **The public narrative.** "What's the version of this story you tell investors, press, customers? The polished one. Why does it land?"

3. **The private truth.** "What's the part of the story you don't tell publicly but is true? The struggle, the pivot you don't talk about, the cofounder who left, the early failure, the mistake that shaped the business?"
   - If they're guarded, reassure: "This file stays on your laptop. The point is your AI tools should know the real story so they don't generate fluff that contradicts it."

4. **Pivot moments.** "Walk me through the major pivots — the moments the business changed direction. What forced each one? What did you learn?"

5. **The voice for telling it.** "When you tell this story at its best — what's the tone? Confident and dry? Vulnerable and warm? Provocative? Plain-spoken? Capture the voice so any AI tool writing in your name uses it."

6. **What you want this story to mean in 5 years.** "When you look back at this in 5 years, what's the meaning you want to take from it? What's the chapter title?"

**Stop condition:** You have an origin story, the public version, the private truth, at least one pivot moment, a defined voice, and a forward-looking meaning.

**Judgment-layer challenge:**
> *Authenticity lens. Read the public narrative back and ask: "Does this match the private truth, or does it dodge it? If they conflict, the public narrative will eventually break under scrutiny. Where's the gap and how do you want to close it?"*
